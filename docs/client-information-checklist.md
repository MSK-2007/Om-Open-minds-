## 1. Instructions for the Client / School Leadership

This document outlines the authentic school information, verified copy, and authorized digital assets required to populate the **OM OPEN MINDS** web platform.

**Guiding Principles:**
1. **Mandatory vs. Optional:** Items marked **[MANDATORY]** are technically or legally essential for launching the public website. Items marked **[OPTIONAL]** can be populated later via the administrative dashboard.
2. **Authenticity:** Please provide genuine institutional records. Do not invent details; where a specific policy or credential does not exist, leave it blank or note "Not Applicable".
3. **Child Safeguarding:** Any photographs containing student faces must be accompanied by signed parental/guardian media release consent forms.

---

## 2. Institutional Information Checklist

### A. Core Institutional Identity
- [ ] **[MANDATORY] Official School Name:** Exact legal name for registration, header, and copyright (e.g., "OM OPEN MINDS").
- [ ] **[MANDATORY] Institutional Tagline:** Concise motto or descriptor (e.g., "Specialized Education for Children with Diverse Learning Needs").
- [ ] **[MANDATORY] Official Vector Logo:** Scalable vector format (`.svg` or high-resolution `.png` with transparent background). Dark-mode / light-mode variations if available.
- [ ] **[OPTIONAL] Institutional Favicon:** 32x32 px or 64x64 px icon asset.
- [ ] **[MANDATORY] Trust / Society Backing:** Legal governing entity name and formal registration / society numbers (if applicable).

### B. Contact & Physical Navigation Channels
- [ ] **[MANDATORY] Primary Campus Address:** Complete physical street address, building/campus name, city, district, and postal code.
- [ ] **[MANDATORY] Direct Phone Numbers:** Primary administrative office phone number.
- [ ] **[MANDATORY] Emergency / Parent Helpline Phone:** Rapid-contact phone number for urgent family matters.
- [ ] **[MANDATORY] Official Inquiry Email Address:** Monitored administrative email address (e.g., `info@omopenminds.example` or `admissions@omopenminds.example`).
- [ ] **[MANDATORY] Office Operating & Visiting Hours:** Exact days and times when campus visits and phone inquiries are accepted (e.g., "Mon–Fri: 9:00 AM – 3:30 PM (By Appointment)").
- [ ] **[MANDATORY] Barrier-Free Campus Access Notes:** Specific instructions for visitors requiring accessibility accommodations (e.g., ramp entrance location, accessible parking bays, elevator access, door widths).
- [ ] **[OPTIONAL] Social Media Profiles:** Official institutional handles (Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube, Instagram).

### C. Institutional Narrative, Vision & Mission
- [ ] **[MANDATORY] Founding Story & Heritage:** 2–4 paragraphs detailing why OM OPEN MINDS was created, the founder's inspiration, and the milestones achieved to date.
- [ ] **[MANDATORY] Vision Statement:** Long-term societal vision for inclusive education and student independence.
- [ ] **[MANDATORY] Mission Statement:** Daily educational commitment to nurturing every child's individual strengths.
- [ ] **[MANDATORY] Core Institutional Values:** 4–6 foundational principles (e.g., Dignity, Multidisciplinary Teamwork, Child-Paced Growth, Family Partnership).

### D. Leadership Desk
- [ ] **[MANDATORY] Director / Principal Full Name & Credentials:** Full name, degrees, certifications (e.g., RCI registration, M.Ed. Special Education).
- [ ] **[MANDATORY] Leadership Welcome Address:** 3–5 paragraphs welcoming families, addressing parental anxieties, and sharing the school's commitment.
- [ ] **[MANDATORY] Leadership Portrait Photograph:** Professional, high-resolution portrait photograph.
- [ ] **[OPTIONAL] Digital Signature Graphic:** Transparent PNG of director's signature.

### E. Educational Programmes & Curricula
- [ ] **[MANDATORY] Formal Program Names:** Exact titles for each developmental stage offered (e.g., Early Intervention, Foundation, Life Skills, Pre-Vocational).
- [ ] **[MANDATORY] Target Readiness / Developmental Criteria:** Clear description of which children benefit most from each pathway (without clinical pigeonholing).
- [ ] **[MANDATORY] Core Focus Areas per Program:** 4–6 bulleted curriculum areas per stage (e.g., Sensory regulation, functional communication, adaptive daily living skills, cognitive fundamentals).
- [ ] **[OPTIONAL] Typical Educator-to-Student Ratios:** e.g., 1:2 or 1:4 per group.
- [ ] **[OPTIONAL] Daily Schedule Format:** Typical daily breakdown of activities and therapy integration.

### F. Teaching Methodology & "Our Approach"
- [ ] **[MANDATORY] Individualized Education Plan (IEP) Process:** Detailed description of how the school formulates, monitors, and reviews student goals with parents.
- [ ] **[MANDATORY] Multidisciplinary Model:** How educators, speech therapists, occupational therapists, and counselors collaborate during school hours.
- [ ] **[MANDATORY] Sensory & Behavioral Support Policy:** Overview of how sensory breaks, quiet rooms, and positive behavior supports are implemented.
- [ ] **[MANDATORY] Milestone Review Frequency:** How often formal parent consultations take place (e.g., quarterly review).

### G. Campus Facilities & Specialized Infrastructure
- [ ] **[MANDATORY] Inventory of Rooms & Spaces:** Names of specialized suites (e.g., Sensory Integration Room, Speech Therapy Suite, Occupational Therapy Gym, Calm Down Space, Adaptive Playground).
- [ ] **[MANDATORY] Facility Photographs:** High-resolution photographs of each room showing physical setup, lighting, and safety adaptations.
- [ ] **[MANDATORY] Descriptions for Alt Text:** 2–3 sentences per facility describing the room layout and equipment for screen-reader users.
- [ ] **[MANDATORY] Safety & Hygiene Checklist:** Specific safety adaptations in place (e.g., padded floors, round-edged furniture, CCTV in public areas, fire exits).

### H. Staff & Faculty Profiles
- [ ] **[MANDATORY] Multidisciplinary Faculty Roster:** Full names, roles, departments, formal educational qualifications, and professional registrations for staff who will appear on the site.
- [ ] **[OPTIONAL] Staff Short Bios:** 2–3 sentences on background and philosophy.
- [ ] **[OPTIONAL] Staff Portrait Photos:** Professional standardized photographs (with staff consent).

### I. Activities, Events & Student Life
- [ ] **[MANDATORY] Weekly Extracurricular Activities:** Regular sessions conducted (e.g., Adaptive Yoga, Music & Movement, Expressive Art, Sensory Gardening).
- [ ] **[OPTIONAL] Annual Events Calendar:** Dates for Sports Day, Annual Day, Disability Awareness Days, Parent Workshops.
- [ ] **[OPTIONAL] Activity Photographs:** Consented photographs of activities and student artwork.

### J. Admissions & Consultation Journey
- [ ] **[MANDATORY] Approved 3-Step Consultation Description:** Official description of the initial phone consultation, campus observation, and enrollment roadmap.
- [ ] **[MANDATORY] Admissions Contact Email & Lead Recipient:** Staff email address that will receive notification alerts when a parent submits an inquiry.
- [ ] **[MANDATORY] Common Admissions FAQs:** 5–8 questions parents frequently ask regarding fees, transport, age criteria, and trial sessions, with approved answers.

### K. Parent Resources
- [ ] **[OPTIONAL] Downloadable Academic Calendar:** PDF file of current school year dates and holidays.
- [ ] **[OPTIONAL] Parent Handbook / Guidelines:** PDF document detailing school policies and daily expectations.
- [ ] **[OPTIONAL] Home Sensory / Routine Tips:** 2–3 short guide documents for parents.

### L. Testimonials & Parent Voices
- [ ] **[OPTIONAL] Verified Parent Testimonials:** 3–5 written feedback statements from current families highlighting progress and emotional trust.
- [ ] **[MANDATORY] Testimonial Consent:** Explicit confirmation of whether the family wishes their name published or prefers an anonymous label (e.g., *"Parent of 6-year-old student"*).

### M. Legal, Safety & Privacy Policies
- [ ] **[MANDATORY] Child Safeguarding Policy:** Official school statement regarding child safety and reporting protocols.
- [ ] **[MANDATORY] Digital Privacy Policy:** Policy explaining how parent inquiry contact details are stored, used, and protected.
- [ ] **[MANDATORY] Terms of Website Use & Copyright:** Standard institutional disclaimers.
